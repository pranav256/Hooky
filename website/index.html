Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about setting up and running Hooky.

Do I need an expensive NVIDIA GPU to run Hooky?

Not at all! While Hooky automatically detects NVIDIA CUDA GPUs for blazing-fast NVENC encoding and whisper acceleration, it includes adaptive low-spec fallbacks that run efficiently on standard CPUs and Apple Silicon (Mac M1/M2/M3) using compute=int8.

How much does the Google Gemini API cost?

Google provides a generous free tier for Gemini 2.0 Flash in Google AI Studio. Because Hooky only passes transcript text (not massive raw video files) to Gemini, analysis uses minimal tokens and typically stays well within Google's free usage quota.

How does the Anti-Ban Instagram Publisher work?

Instagram flags rapid bot-like uploads. Hooky implements human-paced publishing: configurable minimum gap (e.g. 30 minutes), randomized jitter delays (up to 2 hours), and active posting hour constraints (e.g. 9 AM - 10 PM) with checkpoint recovery.

Can I customize the subtitle fonts and styles?

Yes! Hooky comes pre-loaded with three distinct templates: hormozi (viral punchy karaoke), clean (minimalist podcast), and yellow. You can easily tweak font size, margin, colors, and border widths in your .env configuration.
